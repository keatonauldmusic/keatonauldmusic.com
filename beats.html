All commissioned beats are crafted entirely to your specifications, based on any references and details you choose to provide. However, you can still choose your level of investment, which will define certain features such as how many revisions I offer before the beat is final, and whether or not I am free to upload the beat to my beat library after your purchase (exclusivity). Each of these features is individually negotiable, but the options below should give you a basic idea of the choice you have.

Each of the options below can be assumed to have a Producing/Publishing split of 50/50 unless some other agreement is made.

*A Note on Airbit: Please note that purchase options and contract agreements listed on airbit.com differ from those listed above. These purchase options stand for any Keaton Auld beat published through Airbit. However, exclusive licenses are not available for purchase through Airbit. To request an exclusive license to a beat found on Airbit, simply follow the instructions above for requesting an exclusive license, and provide a link to the airbit.com page of the beat. Note additionally that because beats that are already in the Keaton Auld library are not custom commissions, exclusive licenses can be purchased at a negotiable discount. However, be aware that because beats posted on Airbit have been available for lease, you are not guaranteed sole publishing rights over any individuals who may have already purchased a lease.
